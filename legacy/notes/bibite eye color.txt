The eye color is the color on the opposite of the (RGB) color spectrum from the Bibites body color. 
For example, a blue Bibite will have yellow eyes (red + green => yellow). 
If the Bibites color is close to a grayscale color, this does become a bit more complicated: the game will take the most dominant color component(s) and take the opposite of that.
For example, a Bibite with color r: 0.1, g: 0.11, b: 0.1 will be assumed to be green, and the eyes will have the corresponding color purple. 
If they are exactly identical, the Bibite is assumed to be red and has cyan eyes.
